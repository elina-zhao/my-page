How it works

Your assistant reaches Podbean through the open Model Context Protocol.

1

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any other assistant that supports MCP. Add Podbean from your assistant's connector settings — it takes about a minute.

Ask in plain language

Say what should happen with your podcast in your own words — the way you'd explain it to a colleague. No dashboard, no forms.

Frequently asked questions

What is MCP?

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol — an open standard that lets AI assistants use outside tools and services. Podbean's MCP server is what lets your assistant act on your podcast: publishing, editing, chapters and analytics all run through it.

Which AI assistants can I use with Podbean MCP?

Any assistant that supports MCP, including Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. You add Podbean from your assistant's connector settings, sign in to Podbean, and choose the channel you want it to work with.

Which Podbean plan do I need?

Unlimited Audio or above. You also need at least one public podcast channel, and AI features such as automatic show notes and chapter generation draw on your Podbean AI credits.

Can my assistant publish without asking me?

No. Podbean creates a draft first, and an episode only goes live after you explicitly approve and publish it. Deleting an episode also requires a confirmation step — so a misread instruction can never take your show off the feed.

Can I use Podbean MCP with more than one podcast?

Each authorization grants access to one podcast channel. If you run several shows, connect the one you want to work with, and switch channels whenever you need to.

How far back does the analytics data go?

Daily figures cover the last 90 days and monthly figures the last 24 months, with roughly a one-day lag before the most recent numbers appear. You can ask for downloads, trends, and breakdowns by country, app or source.

What does Podbean AI do in the workflow?

When you publish, Podbean AI can transcribe the audio and draft a title, show notes, chapter markers and a transcript. You can also have it clean up the sound with noise reduction, leveling and voice clarity — and edit anything it writes before publishing.

Do I still need the Podbean dashboard?

Not for everyday work. Publishing, scheduling, show notes, chapters, profile changes and analytics can all be handled in the chat. The dashboard is still there whenever you'd rather click than talk.

Can I see my show's embed player from the chat?

Yes. Ask your assistant for the embed code for an episode and it will hand you the player markup to drop into your site.

Is there a step-by-step setup guide?

Podbean's help center walks through the whole thing, from connecting your assistant to publishing your first episode: Read the setup guide. It uses Claude as the example, but the steps are the same in any MCP client.
