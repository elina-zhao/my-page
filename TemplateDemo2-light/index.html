Be a Guest on Meditation Studio

We love sharing guided meditations, breathing exercises, and mindfulness practices with our listeners. If you are a teacher, coach, or practitioner with something valuable to offer, we would love to have you on the show.

What we are looking for

  • Guided meditations and breathing exercises you can lead live
  • Practical tips on sleep, focus, or stress that listeners can use today
  • A calm, clear way of explaining ideas - no prior experience required to apply

How it works

  1. Tell us a little about yourself and what you would like to share.
  2. We will schedule a short call to plan your episode together.
  3. Record at your convenience - we handle the editing and publishing.
