FAQ

Questions, answered

What Google data does Podbean access?

Just two things: your basic profile (name and email) to link your account, and permission to upload videos to the YouTube channel you select. We don’t read your email, Drive, or any other Google service, and you can disconnect at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

Which Podbean plan includes Share to YouTube?

Share to YouTube is available from your Podbean dashboard under Distribution. The exact plan availability is shown there — log in and look for “Share to YouTube” to get started.

Do I need my own YouTube channel?

Yes. You connect a YouTube channel that you own and control. Podbean only publishes to the channel you authorize during setup.

What does the video actually look like?

Each video uses your episode artwork as the visual, layered with your show title, episode name, and a clean playback layout. You can add captions and the description is copied from your episode notes automatically.

Can I review episodes before they’re published?

Yes. Keep auto-publish off and new episodes land in your drafts, where you can watch, edit, and schedule them. Turn auto-publish on any time to skip that step.

What happens to episodes I’ve already published?

Nothing changes on your existing YouTube channel. If you connect one that already has uploads, they stay untouched, and you can choose whether to bring your older Podbean episodes over.

Can I turn it off after connecting?

Anytime. Disconnect the channel from your Podbean settings and nothing further will be published. It only takes one click.
