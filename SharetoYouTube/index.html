Your podcast, on YouTube. Automatically.

Each new episode becomes a clean video — your artwork, your audio — and is uploaded to the YouTube channel you choose the moment it goes live.

Share video to YouTube in three steps

Set it up once. Every episode after that is handled for you.

1

Connect your channel

In your Podcast Dashboard, go to Distribution → Social Share. Connect YouTube and pick the YouTube channel you want to publish to.

2

We make the video

Each new episode becomes a clean video — your artwork, episode title, and audio. New episodes auto-share, and you can also share any episode manually or download its video.

3

It goes live

When you publish an episode, its video goes live on your chosen channel automatically — no extra work. Toggle sharing or edit video details any time.

Frequently asked questions

Podbean Video Sharing requests only what it needs to publish for you.

What Google data does Podbean access?

Only what it needs to publish for you. After you authorize in your Dashboard, Podbean turns each episode's audio into a video and uploads it to the YouTube channel you choose. To do that it asks for three permissions: youtube.upload — upload the video Podbean creates; youtube.readonly — confirm your channel exists and read its channel ID; and userinfo.profile — get your Google user ID and display name.

Why do you need my Google profile?

To link your Google account to Podbean so we can identify you and show which Google account and YouTube channel are connected.

Why do you need to upload to my channel?

To publish the video Podbean builds from your episode — only to the channel you select, and only when you publish an episode.

What does Podbean store when I connect a channel?

The connection details it needs to publish for you: an OAuth access and refresh token, your Google user ID and display name, your channel ID, and the connection status.

Can I stop sharing anytime?

Yes. Toggle sharing per episode, or disconnect YouTube from Podbean settings whenever you like.
