Connect your own domain to give your podcast website a professional, branded URL. How to set up
SSL certificate will be automatically provisioned for your domain.
How to set up your own domain
1
Purchase a domain
If you don't already have one, buy a domain from any registrar such as Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains.
2
Add a CNAME record
In your domain registrar's DNS settings, create a CNAME record pointing your domain (e.g. podcast.yourdomain.com) to custom.podbean.com.
3
Enter your domain above
Type your domain in the field above and click Save. SSL will be provisioned automatically within a few minutes.
