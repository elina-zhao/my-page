Claim your podcast on Apple Podcasts →
1. Log in to your Apple Podcasts Connect account. If you don't have an Apple account, you can create one.
2. At the bottom of the page, under More Actions, click Claim Existing Show.
3. Copy your Apple Podcast URL
4. Follow the prompts to find your show and generate a token. The token is valid for seven days.
5. Copy the claim token from Apple, paste it below
The token will be added to your RSS feed. Within 24 hours, Apple Podcasts will verify it matches the one you generated in Apple Podcasts Connect and will automatically move your show to your account.
Enable HLS Video →
Deliver high-quality video streaming to Apple Podcasts
Authorize Podbean to publish your video episodes to Apple Podcasts in HLS video format.
1. Log in to Apple Podcasts Connect.
2. Make sure {podcast name} is listed in your Apple Podcasts Connect account. If it is not listed, please claim your podcast first and wait 24 hours.
3. Go to Account > Keys.
4. Click the + icon next to Active.
5. Enter a name for the API key, such as Podbean.
6. Click Generate, then click Download. Do not rename the downloaded file.
7. Upload your API key file to Podbean.

Offer premium content through Apple Podcasts Subscriptions
1. Publish at least one audio episode.
2. Make sure {podcast name} is listed in your Apple Podcasts Connect account. If it is not listed, please claim your podcast first and wait 24 hours.
3. Sign up for Apple Podcasts Subscriptions and create your subscription.
4. Download your Apple Podcasts API Key from Apple. Learn more.
5. Upload your Apple Podcasts API Key to connect your Apple Podcasts account with Podbean.
